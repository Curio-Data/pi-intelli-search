>
> D2) Approve Herdr builders for isolated helper modules, with central integration remaining coordinator-owned?

> D1) yes
>

Recorded on 21 October 2026. This decision adopts [Delivery Milestones](DELIVERY-PLAN.md), not a feature or release verdict.

## Sequencing Interpretation

The P4 research remainder and P5 are co-delivered as M2, with joint acceptance of the applicable result/schema/producer and pipeline-integration obligations. Integrate the accepted P1/P2/P4 service/interface prerequisites before wiring the replacement research pipeline; record phase exit only after its applicable proofs pass. Do not implement a temporary legacy research producer solely to satisfy an artificial delivery boundary.

The frozen behaviour, public signatures, accounting, ownership, cancellation, persistence and acceptance rows remain unchanged. No shared prerequisite moves into native-only R2. If implementation requires a conflicting semantic or interface change, use the specification-revision procedure rather than silently alter the frozen bundle.

## Delegation

Builders may implement isolated new deadline, staging, publication and qualification modules with dedicated tests and coordinator-defined internal contracts. The coordinator retains central contracts/settings/schemas, native seams, adapter cutover and `research.ts` integration, plus combined acceptance. Use _Claude Code_ with Opus 5.5 and `Pi` with GLM 5.3 for implementation support. No other model is a silent substitute.

Use two implementation support slots and a serial validation lane. Do not share mutable worktree state, run competing full gates or delegate central integration through an isolated-file brief.

## Unchanged Approvals

Standing mandatory `01_main.sh` approval stays with the unfinished `feat/codemode-research` branch. This decision does not transfer that approval to builder branches or approve studies, other live scenarios, staging, publication or merging. Final independent reviews, paid-tranche decisions, candidate/release ownership and the R1/R2 split remain unchanged.

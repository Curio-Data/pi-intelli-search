# M4 and Cache Cleanup, 4 November 2026

This page records the second approved pass. [Study Closure](CLOSURE.md) records the final worktree retirement. Ignored evidence paths below are historical and no longer exist after that retirement; the historical source changes survive as [a compact patch](archive/).

The second owner-approved cleanup removed `.tmp/codemode-m4/` and the exact cache-only candidates identified after the [first cleanup](CLEANUP-20261104.md). This pass was limited to the `codemode-research` worktree. Its allocated footprint fell from 18.90 GiB to 7.58 GiB, a net reduction of 11.32 GiB after retention. Remaining scratch is 2.09 GiB.

These figures measure directory allocation, not exclusive storage or physical reclamation. Filesystem available space decreased during the operation; shared extents, snapshots, newly retained copies and concurrent host activity prevent attributing that change solely to cleanup.

## Authority and Scope

The owner approved removal of `.tmp/codemode-m4/`, then agreed to the other cleanup suggestions. The disclosed interpretation of that second instruction was the exact cache-only list in `evidence/codemode/20261104T115444Z-approved-cleanup/branch-narrow-cache-candidates-20261104T125004Z.json`, not blanket removal of agent scratch or other checkouts.

The cache list contained 30 npm content-cache roots and 194 compiler-cache roots. Forty-four were inside the M4 directory. The deletion therefore removed 181 non-overlapping roots: M4 plus 180 external cache roots. npm logs outside M4 remained in place; logs inside M4 were retained in the archive described below. No main-checkout scratch, other worktree, process, raw benchmark or publication output was selected for removal.

Raw benchmarks are deferred until other cleanup is complete and a separate agent has assessed their suitability. This is a sequencing decision, not a permanent prohibition on owner-approved disposal. No benchmark disposal was authorised or performed in this pass.

## Retained Material

The M4 directory contained a separate Git snapshot with staged and unstaged changes. Removing it as disposable scratch would have discarded source state. Before deletion, the complete snapshot was copied to `evidence/codemode/20261104T135327Z-m4-cache-cleanup/retained/source-snapshot/`, including its Git metadata. Its 29,833 regular files were hash-verified, symlink targets were checked, and its staged and unstaged status matched the original after removal. This standalone snapshot was not one of the registered linked worktrees.

All other M4 material, except the approved caches and five transient authentication files, was retained in `evidence/codemode/20261104T135327Z-m4-cache-cleanup/retained/m4-noncache.tar.zst`. The archive contains 149,421 members, including 133,220 regular files. Every file digest, symlink target and member mode was verified against the original inventory before deletion. It retains source, summaries, successful and failed attempts, historical receipts, logs, private host installations and dependency inputs without selecting only favourable outcomes. The authentication files were not copied; their paths and disposal are recorded without their contents.

The retained snapshot occupies 1.24 GiB and the archive 1.32 GiB. The archive preserves historical material conservatively, including installed dependencies; it is not a claim that every retained byte must remain indefinitely. These ignored local records remained in the worktree until its owner-approved retirement and were not distributed with a clean clone. No external backup or restore was assumed or relied upon.

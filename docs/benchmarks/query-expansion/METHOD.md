# Query Expansion Method

Method revision: `P0-1`. Status: unexecuted exploratory method. [Common rules](../README.md) govern recording, replay, judging, missingness and budget. Exact original queries, three expansions, domains, focuses and rubrics are in the [corpus](../corpus/query-expansion.json) and hashed [manifest](../corpus/manifest.json).

## Inputs and Arms

Expansions are coordinator-authored and frozen, not generated by a paid model in this method. Do not attribute results to an automatic expansion model. Any later generated-expansion arm needs its own method/input revision and cost accounting before execution.

| Arm | Queries | Attempted-Page Request |
|---|---|---|
| E1 | Original only | 10 |
| E2 | Original + first expansion | 10 per query |
| E3 | Original + first two expansions | 10 per query |
| E4 | Original + all three expansions | 10 per query |
| E3-Serial | Exact same three E3 queries | 10 per query, serial execution |
| E1-Budget | Original only | 30 in one search |

Set and record effective `maxUrls >= 30` for these arms. The default cap cannot stand in for E1-Budget. Record requested, effective, returned, attempted, successfully fetched/extracted and unique pages; a single search returning fewer usable links is an observed shortfall, not a thirty-page observation. Do not top it up with unrecorded queries. E1-Budget and E3 match attempted-page allowance, not successful-page count or dollars.

All arms use the same fixed domain/focus settings and model/token policies. Expanded terms are source acquisition treatments; deduplicate URL/content identities with an explicit common rule. Keep any realised-success-matched secondary comparison labelled secondary and dependent on the E3 observation that selected its budget.

## Execution

Compare E3 and E3-Serial under complete recorded-result replay first, with deadline disabled, to isolate scheduling on fixed evidence. Native core concurrency and actual parallel MCP protocol operations are separate adapter checks; a process-local semaphore alone does not establish protocol overlap. Record aggregate limits, critical reserve and per-run bounds.

Report live acquisition/routing differences separately from replay results. A live novel argument is not penalised by a fixed fixture dictionary; forced replay must use the frozen supported arguments and stop on a miss. No record-on-first-use series is called independent confirmatory replay.

## Endpoints

Measure source-pool-relative recall, reference-fact/rubric coverage, redundancy, successful evidence, latency and paid pipeline cost. The observed pool is not exhaustive web relevance ground truth; report the denominator and sensitivity to pooling. Never assert a universal direction of absolute-recall bias from that metric.

Produce one final answer per arm under the same final-synthesis token budget and main model. The union of several intermediate summaries is not the final-answer quality endpoint. Both blinded judges score that common output against captured evidence/rubrics; keep their disagreements and same-model-judge sensitivity.

Count all actual search/extract/collate/suggestion, retries/failures and any pipeline synthesis in OpenRouter cost. Coordinator-authored expansion generation has no model charge here, but main/final-synthesis subscription tokens/quota still count as resource observations. Equal pages are not equal dollars.

## Decision Boundary

Use development/pilot data to assess whether extra acquisition improves coverage/usefulness enough to justify observed resource cost. Freeze any selected policy before held-out evaluation. Keep independent query-level observations and all failed/shortfall runs. Do not claim a universal best expansion count, non-inferiority, equivalence or confirmatory precision from this proposed small pilot; D6 and a separately approved independent series govern those claims.
